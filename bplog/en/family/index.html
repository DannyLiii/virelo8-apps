Family care (planned)

We're considering adding family care to BP Notebook, and we'd like to know first how many people need it.

What it would do

  • A parent logs blood pressure on their own iPhone, and their children see it on theirs.
  • iPhones are paired with a QR code. There's no account to sign up for.
  • Family members are told when a reading hasn't been logged yet, or when one is very high.
  • One person subscribes, and up to 6 family members use it together.

The plan is to encrypt the numbers on the parent's iPhone so that only family members' iPhones can read them. We can't see them either.

Add your vote

The vote is being set up. Once it opens, a button will appear here. It asks one question, how many family members you'd like to follow, with no sign-in and no email address.
