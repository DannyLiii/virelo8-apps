Questions

Does the app measure blood pressure?

No. An iPhone can't measure blood pressure. Measure with your own monitor and log the numbers in the app, or let your monitor's app save them to Apple Health and they'll come in by themselves.

How do readings from my monitor come in automatically?

Turn on writing to Health in your monitor's own app, then connect in Settings > Apple Health in this app. From then on, blood pressure saved to Health shows up in the app by itself.

Can I photograph my monitor to enter a reading?

Yes, on iPhones with iOS 27 and Apple Intelligence turned on (iPhone 15 Pro or later). Tap "Photograph the blood pressure monitor" at the top of the entry screen and take a picture, or pick one from Photos. The app reads it twice on your iPhone and marks any number the two reads disagree on, so check before you tap Save. The photo is read on your iPhone and is never uploaded or kept. On other iPhones, pick the numbers on the wheels as usual.

Can I log with Siri?

Yes. Say "Hey Siri, log blood pressure in BP Notebook": Siri asks for the top and bottom numbers, saves the reading and tells you its level, and what to do right away when it's very high. Your iPhone needs to be unlocked to add a reading. The Shortcuts app has a Log blood pressure action too.

How do I show my readings to family?

Tap share on the 7-day average card on Today. The app turns the last 3 days (7 with Pro) into one "Blood pressure summary" picture you can send by Messages or any app, or save to Photos. The picture is made on your iPhone and goes only where you send it.

Why is my level different from what my doctor said?

The app uses the published guideline for your App Store region, named in Settings > Reference standard and on every report. Your doctor sets targets for you personally, so follow your doctor's advice; you can also set the target your doctor gave you in the app.

How do I move my readings to a new iPhone?

They move with your iPhone when you transfer it using iCloud Backup or a computer backup. With Pro, open Settings > Move to a new iPhone on the old iPhone, then open the app on the new one, choose Move from your old iPhone and scan the code. Readings, doctor's notes with photos, medications, reminders and settings all move. If the old iPhone isn't with you, make a file in Settings > Export and backup > Make a backup file and use Restore from backup file on the new iPhone. Restoring is free.

My alarm reminder didn't ring

Alarm reminders need iOS 26.1 or later, and the app must be allowed to schedule alarms in Settings > BP Notebook. Without that, reminders come as notifications. Once you've logged that time of day, it stays quiet that day.

How do I cancel a subscription or restore a purchase?

Cancel: in the app, open Settings, tap Pro at the top, choose Cancel subscription, then Cancel with Apple. You can also cancel in iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and your readings are kept.

Refunds: the same screen has Request a refund, which opens Apple's refund sheet. Apple decides whether to refund.

Restore: after moving to a new iPhone or reinstalling, tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.
