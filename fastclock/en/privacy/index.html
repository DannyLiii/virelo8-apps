Privacy Policy

Fasting Clock doesn't collect any of your personal data. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and the app doesn't send your data to any server.

  • No account
  • No data collected
  • No tracking or ads
  • Your data stays on your iPhone

Who this policy covers

The iPhone and Apple Watch app "Fasting Clock" (the app), provided by the individual developer LI YAN-SHAN.

Data kept on your iPhone

Each fast you record (start and end times, plan, how you felt, notes and check-ins during the fast), your plans and reminders, weight and water, meal photos and their one-line descriptions, the names of your skies, your challenge progress (including which fasts counted), your first ring (which fasts and when it was completed), which year's sky you've opened, and your settings are kept only in the app's storage on your iPhone. Home Screen widgets, the Live Activity on the Lock Screen and the Apple Watch show the time of your fast; this data passes only between your iPhone and your Apple Watch, and we never receive it.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this data is included in your own device backup as iOS is set up to do. We have no access to your iPhone or your backups.

Your answers

To build a plan and choose the right reminders, the app asks a few questions the first time: sex, age, height and weight, how active you are, your health (for example pregnancy or breastfeeding, an eating disorder, diabetes and medication), your eating habits and your fasting experience. For some answers the app suggests asking a doctor first and switches to a safe mode that only times and records.

Your answers stay on your iPhone, in backup files you make yourself and on a new iPhone you move to. They don't appear in notifications or widgets, the app doesn't send them anywhere else, and we can't see them.

Apple Health

The app reads and writes Health data only after you allow it, and you can allow just some of it.

  • Read: weight, body fat, lean body mass, waist and height, and the heart rate and rhythm notifications your Apple Watch sends. With Pro, also sleep, steps, exercise, active energy, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and blood glucose from a glucose monitor, shown beside each fast.
  • Written: the weight and water you log in the app.

When your Apple Watch sends a heart rate notification during a fast, the app asks how you feel. The app only shows these numbers; it doesn't analyse them or give medical advice.

This data is used only on your iPhone to show it, never for advertising, never shared and never uploaded. You can turn access off at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

Camera and photos

When you take a photo for "this meal", the app uses the camera (asking the first time). You can also pick a photo instead; the app reads only the one you pick, without access to your photo library. Photos are kept in the app, not in your photo library, and are never uploaded. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, the photo is read on the iPhone itself by its built-in Apple Intelligence to write one line about what you ate.

Moving to a new iPhone and backup files

When you open Settings > New phone > Move to a new phone on your old iPhone, the app first checks it's you with Face ID or your passcode. iOS tells the app only whether this succeeded; the app never receives your face data. Your old iPhone then shows a QR code that works for 2 minutes and only once.

Your new iPhone scans the code with its camera (asking the first time), and the two iPhones connect on the same Wi-Fi network, or directly when they're close (asking for local network access the first time). Both show the same four digits, and nothing is sent until you confirm on the old iPhone. What moves (your fasts, plans and settings including your answers, reminders, the weight and water that aren't in Health, meal photos and their descriptions, the names of your skies, and your challenges, constellations and rings) is encrypted with a key made for this move only and goes straight from one iPhone to the other, through no server and not through iCloud. The connection closes when the move is done. Data already in Health is synced by Apple.

Backup files hold the same data, encrypted with a password you choose, and are saved where you pick in the Files app. If you save one to iCloud Drive, Apple's terms apply. We never receive your backup files and can't recover your password.

Reminders

Notifications are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone, without any server. The safety reminder at 24 hours is a Time Sensitive notification, so it also appears during a Focus.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Control Center

You can start or end a fast and log a glass of water with Siri, Shortcuts, Home Screen widgets and Control Center. These actions are handled on your iPhone. When you use Siri, what you say is processed by Apple's Siri under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

Sky pictures and wallpapers are made on your iPhone and leave it only when you share or save them yourself, with whoever you choose. A picture shows only the month, its name and the stars, never hours or weight.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple; we never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether you have Pro. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Crashes and diagnostics

If you choose to share with app developers in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple provides crash reports and usage statistics without your identity, which we use only to fix bugs.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete any fast in History, and undo it right after.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Weight and water already written to Health are deleted in the Health app.

Children

The app is made for adults; anyone under 18 should ask a doctor before fasting. We don't collect anyone's data, children included.

Your rights

Because we don't collect your personal data, there's no data of yours for us to look up, correct or delete. If you have any question about privacy, please email us.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we'll update this page and the date below first. If we ever start collecting any data, we'll tell you in the app and ask for your consent first.
