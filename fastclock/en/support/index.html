Safety comes first

Fasting isn't for everyone. Ask a doctor first if you:

  • Are pregnant, breastfeeding or trying to conceive
  • Are under 18
  • Have had an eating disorder
  • Have diabetes, especially if you take insulin or other medicine that lowers blood sugar
  • Have another long-term illness, take regular medication, or are underweight

At 24 hours the app reminds you to talk to a doctor first, and ending then brings no celebration. If your answers mention pregnancy or an eating disorder, or your BMI is under 18.5, the app switches to a safe mode that only times and records. If you feel dizzy, your heart races or you feel very unwell during a fast, stop, eat and get help if needed.

FAQ

Who shouldn't fast?

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding or trying to conceive, under 18, have had an eating disorder, have diabetes (especially with insulin or other medicine that lowers blood sugar), have another long-term illness, take regular medication or are underweight, ask a doctor first. The same list is in the app under Settings > Who should ask a doctor first.

I forgot to tap start. What now?

On Today, tap Started earlier?, or during a fast tap Change the start, and pick when you really started. You can change the end time later too.

Does it keep counting past my goal?

Yes. The dial counts until you tap end. At 24 hours the app reminds you to talk to a doctor first, and ending then brings no celebration, just a line about looking after yourself.

What is safe mode?

If your answers mention pregnancy or an eating disorder, or your BMI is under 18.5, the app suggests asking a doctor first and switches to safe mode. You can still time, change times and see your records, but it won't suggest a plan, send encouragement or stage notifications, or show weight targets. After talking with your doctor, tap I've checked with my doctor: back to normal in Settings.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. The weight and water you log are saved to Health, and the weight your scale or other apps save to Health comes in too. With Pro, sleep, steps, exercise, heart rate and a glucose monitor's readings appear beside each fast. The app only shows these numbers; it doesn't analyse them.

Does the Apple Watch app cost extra?

No, the Apple Watch app is free. See the time left on your wrist, start and end a fast, and log a glass of water.

Are my meal photos uploaded?

No. Photos are kept in the app, not in your photo library, and are never uploaded. On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, the photo is read on the iPhone itself to write one line about what you ate.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Your fasts move with your iPhone when you transfer it using iCloud Backup or a computer backup. With Pro, open Settings > New phone > Move to a new phone on the old iPhone, then open the app on the new one, choose Move from your old phone and scan the code. Your fasts, plans, settings and meal photos all move. If the old iPhone isn't with you, make a file in Settings > New phone > Make a backup file and use Restore a backup file on the new iPhone. Restoring is free.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

To cancel, tap Pro at the top of the app's Settings, choose Cancel subscription, then Go to Apple to cancel. You can also cancel in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Pro keeps working until the end of the paid period, and your records stay.

Refunds: the same page has Request a refund, which opens Apple's refund page. Apple decides on refunds.

Restore: after a new phone or reinstalling, tap Restore purchases on the Pro screen.
