Privacy Policy

Hugpal doesn't collect any personal data about you or whoever you look after. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and the app doesn't send your data to any server.

  • No account
  • No data collected
  • No tracking, no ads
  • Your data stays on your iPhones

Who this policy covers

The iPhone app Hugpal ("the app"), provided by the individual developer LI YAN-SHAN.

Data stored on your iPhone

The records you log and the record types you add (including who logged them), the names, birthdays, photos and avatars of those you look after, the names, roles and avatars of the people who care with you, the emergency card (such as allergies, medical history, hospital and contacts), help numbers, house rules, supplies, calendar appointments, album photos and videos, and your settings are all stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. What the widgets, Live Activity and controls need is kept in the app's own shared space on your iPhone, which we can't reach.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this data is included in your own device backup according to your iOS settings. We have no access to your iPhone or your backups.

Camera, microphone and photos

The app uses the camera to take photos and videos (for records, handoff cards, the album and avatars) and to scan QR codes, and the microphone when you record a video. iOS asks for permission the first time. When you pick from Photos, the system picker gives the app only the items you choose, so no photo library permission is needed. Photos and videos stay in the app and are never uploaded. A handoff card or album video is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save (add-only; the app doesn't read the library).

Sharing with the people who care with you

Free lets you invite one person who can look only; any other sharing is Pro. When you invite someone, your phone shows a QR code that they scan with their camera, and the two phones connect directly while on the same Wi-Fi or close by (iOS asks for local network permission the first time). Both show the same four digits, and nothing is sent until you confirm. Records are encrypted with a key made for that one session and go straight from one iPhone to the other, through no server, and the connection closes when it's done.

Each person or pet you look after is shared separately. What someone can see and change follows the role you give them (main carer, co-carer or can look only) and its end date, and is handled by the app on both phones. The album isn't shared unless you choose to.

Moving to a new iPhone and backup files

When you open Move to a new iPhone on the old phone, the app first checks it's you with Face ID or your passcode. iOS tells the app only whether the check succeeded; the app never gets your face data. The move works like sharing, directly between the two iPhones.

Backup files are encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you pick in Files. If you save to iCloud Drive, Apple's terms apply. We can't access your backup files and can't recover your password.

Calendar

The app uses your calendars only if you turn on On the iPhone's calendar too (iOS asks for permission the first time). The app makes a calendar of its own on your iPhone for your appointments and removes that whole calendar when you turn the option off. This data stays in your device's calendar and syncs according to your iCloud Calendar settings.

Reminders

Notifications are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone and go through no server.

Handoff cards and sharing

Handoff cards and album videos are made on your iPhone and leave it only when you tap Share or Save, to whoever you choose. You pick which fields a handoff card carries.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Control Center

You can log or start a timer with Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Control Center, all handled on your iPhone. When you use Siri, what you say is processed by Apple's Siri under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether you have Pro. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

Crashes and diagnostics

If you choose to share with app developers in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple gives us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. We use them only to fix bugs.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any record in These days; you can undo right after.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data on your iPhone, along with its notifications, Live Activity and widgets.
  • If you turned on the calendar option, turn it off in the app first so the app's calendar is removed too.
  • Records already sent to someone else's phone are deleted in the app on that phone.

Children

The app is for adults who look after others. Data about a baby or child is entered by the family and stays only on the iPhones of the people who care for them. We don't collect anyone's data, children's included.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data about you or those you look after, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any privacy question, please write to us.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, for example when syncing from anywhere is added, we'll update this page and the date below first. Before collecting any data, we'll tell you in the app and ask for your consent.
