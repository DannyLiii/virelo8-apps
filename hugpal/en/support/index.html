One tap to log

You choose what to log: meals, walks, medicine, temperature or anything else, each a big card on the home screen that logs with one tap. Time the things that need a timer, and change the start while it runs.

Handoff card

What happened since the last handover, with a note and a photo, ready to send by LINE, WhatsApp or Messages. Whoever gets it doesn't need the app.

These days

A 7-day or 30-day timeline, one column a day, readable at a glance. Tap a day for its list.

Emergency card

Allergies, medical history, the usual hospital and who to call first go along with the handoff. Add your own help numbers, and use the checklist the first time someone new helps out.

Calendar and Supplies

Checkups, shots and grooming in one place, with who takes them and a reminder the day before. When diapers, food or medicine run low, whoever buys them hears about it.

An album you can flip

Sorted by month, with stickers, tape, stamps and handwriting. Turn a month or the whole album into a page-flipping video to share.

FAQ

How do I share the care with someone?

Tap Invite in Family, choose whether they are a main carer, a co-carer or can look only, and set an end date if you like. They scan the QR code with their camera, both phones show the same four digits, and once you confirm, the two phones' records are merged. Inviting one person who can look only is free; anything more is Pro, and whoever you invite doesn't pay.

Does the person who gets a handoff card need the app?

No. A handoff card is a picture, so send it by LINE, WhatsApp, Messages or any app and they just open it.

I logged something by mistake. What now?

Right after you log, you can undo it. Later, tap the day in These days: tap a record to change it, or hold it to delete. Deleting can be undone too.

I forgot to stop a timer.

You can change the start and end while timing and when you stop. If a timer has been running a long time when you open the app, it asks if it's still going.

Will the app tell me if things are normal?

No. The app keeps and shares records; it doesn't interpret them or compare with others. Ask a doctor or vet about their health.

Why do I add the help numbers myself?

The app is offered in many countries and every place has different numbers, so you add your own doctor, vet, pharmacy and ambulance and put them in order. The first three show on the emergency card.

Where is my data kept? What about a new phone?

Records, photos and videos stay on this iPhone, with no cloud. When you get a new phone, open Settings > New iPhone and backups > Move to a new iPhone on the old one and scan the QR code with the new one. If the old phone isn't with you, make a password-protected backup first and restore it on the new phone. Moving and backups are free.

Anything to do before deleting the app?

If you turned on On the iPhone's calendar too, turn it off in the app's settings first so the appointments are removed from Calendar. Otherwise they stay in the Calendar app (they have no alarms, so they won't ring). Notifications, the Live Activity and widgets go when the app is deleted. Records are only on this iPhone and are gone once it's deleted, so make a backup or export a CSV first if you need them.

Does deleting the app cancel my subscription?

No. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Cancel: in iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or open subscription management from the app's Pro page. Pro keeps working until the end of the period, and your records stay.

Refunds are handled by Apple; you can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore: after a new phone or reinstalling, tap Restore purchases on the Pro page.
