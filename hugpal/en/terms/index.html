2. Not a medical device, not an emergency service

  • Hugpal records and shares care, whether for a baby, a pet, a grandparent or anyone else you look after. It is not a medical device, doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and doesn't interpret how they are doing. Ask a doctor or vet about their health.
  • Syncing and notifications between family can arrive late or not at all, for example with no internet, a phone turned off, or server maintenance. Don't rely on them for anything urgent. In an emergency, call your local emergency number right away.
  • Help numbers are the ones you add, so please check they're right.
  • Handoff cards and the emergency card summarize what you've logged. Whoever is giving care should judge by how things are at the time.

3. Your data

Your records are stored on your devices and those of the people who care with you. When you care together with family, records pass through our server encrypted. We have no key, can't open the content and can't recover it for you. Please use your iPhone's backups or the app's password-protected backup file. The data about those you look after is provided by you, and the main caregiver decides who is invited and what is shared. Check who you're sending to before you invite someone or share a handoff card or album video, and respect the wishes of other people in your photos.

4. Pro and payment

  • Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription. The main caregiver turns it on and one plan is shared by the whole family. People you invite don't pay, and you can also share it with your family through Apple's Family Sharing. The price shown on the App Store applies, and Apple handles payment.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged to your Apple Account within the 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.
  • If a charge fails, you stay on Pro during Apple's billing grace period.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Free: looking after one on your own, every record and timer, handoff cards, the emergency card, Need help, House rules, Supplies, the calendar and every reminder, your own avatars and record types, one photo album with 30 pages, widgets, Control Center, Siri, CSV export, moving to a new iPhone and backups.
  • Pro: invite family to care together (up to 10 people, each with their own role), sync and notifications through the server, and videos in the album with no page limit.
  • When Pro ends nothing is locked. Records keep syncing and everyone who cares together stays. You just can't invite more people or upload more photos. Photos on the server are deleted after 90 days, while everything on phones stays.
